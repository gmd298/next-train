    Home screen widget

    A widget shows your leave-time countdown right on the home screen. It runs inside the free Scriptable app.

    1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free).
    2. Tap Copy widget below.
    3. Open Scriptable, tap +, paste, then tap Done.
    4. On your home screen, press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, choose Scriptable, and pick the medium size.
    5. Press and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and for Script choose the one you just pasted.

    Good to know: iPhones only refresh widgets every few minutes, so the train times on it can be a little behind (the countdown itself ticks live). For up-to-the-second times, tap the widget to open this app.

    If you change your station, directions or walk time, tap Copy widget again and paste it over the old script in Scriptable.
